\noindent\textbf{Beilinson survey: conductor $<1000$, 100-digit targets.}
We use $\pi c^+(C)c^-(C)L^{\prime\prime}(M_{\mathrm{std}}(C),1)=q\det R(D_i,D_j)$,
where $R(D_i,D_j)$ is the plane-integral matrix with column factors $(L_{D_i},L_{D_j},1)$
and row weights $(1,2\operatorname{Re}(z),|z|^2)$.
For $D=\sum_P a_P P$, $L_D(z)=\sum_{P\text{ finite}}a_P\log|z-u(P)|$ in the chart $v^2=F(u)$.
The periods $c^+(C),c^-(C)$ are the signed maximal minors of $\operatorname{Re}H,\operatorname{Im}H$
for the full-cycle period matrix $H$ in this chart.
The divisors below are the actual coefficient rows of the regulator columns,
in the original LMFDB coordinates of Table~\ref{tab:curve-models}.
The associated classes $\alpha(D)$ are integral.
Packet order means $N=\mathop{\mathrm{lcm}}_P\operatorname{ord}(P-P_0)$ in the Jacobian,
not the number of packet points. A dash means the exact order was not recorded.
The endomorphism column is the LMFDB rational endomorphism algebra label;
\texttt{CM} does not by itself assert that the Jacobian is of CM type.
The column $N_{\mathrm{std}}$ gives the conductor of $L(M_{\mathrm{std}}(C),s)$.
