Regulator matrix for genus 2 curve

For a genus 2 curve C and two points P and Q which become torsion in the Jacobian of C, we evaluate the 3×3 regulator matrix associated with two motivic cohomology classes αP, αQ, and the Tate class.

The curve and its marked points

The real curve

C(ℝ)

We view C as a double cover of ℙ¹ via x : C → ℙ¹. Using this, we cut up the hyperelliptic curve into two simply connected sheets in order to convert the two-dimensional regulator integral to an integral over the cut. By choosing the cut to be the preimage of a convenient tree in the x-plane, we can moreover choose a path B on the tree as the branch cut for the logarithmic factor of the integrand. With this choice, the integral over the cut simplifies to a bilinear expression of line integrals.

The tree in ℙ¹

The vertices are the critical values of x (images of ramification points), x(P), and x(Q). A minimum spanning tree of their complete graph gives the cut.
