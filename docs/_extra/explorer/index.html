We view C as a double cover of ℙ¹ in the cut coordinates (u,v). Using this, we cut up the hyperelliptic curve into two simply connected sheets in order to convert the two-dimensional regulator integral to an integral over the cut. By choosing the cut to be the preimage of a convenient tree in the u-plane, we can moreover choose a path B on the tree as the branch cut for the logarithmic factor of the integrand. With this choice, the integral over the cut simplifies to a bilinear expression of line integrals.
