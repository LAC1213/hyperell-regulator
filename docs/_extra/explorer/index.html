We view C as a double cover of ℙ¹ via x : C → ℙ¹. We lift a well-chosen spanning tree in ℙ¹ to a graph in C, keeping track of the edge orientations on the two sheets. Applying Stokes’ theorem reduces the surface integral to one-dimensional integrals along the edges of this graph.

The graph in ℙ¹

The vertices are the critical values of x (images of ramification points), x(P), and x(Q). A minimum spanning tree of their complete graph gives the cut.
