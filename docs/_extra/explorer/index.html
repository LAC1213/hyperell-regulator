GENUS 2 · BEILINSON REGULATORS

From the curve to its regulator.

Start with a hyperelliptic curve C and two marked points P, Q. We evaluate the regulator matrix of their motivic cohomology classes αP and αQ using Stokes’ theorem.

01 A spanning tree on ℙ¹

The vertices are the critical values of x (images of ramification points), x(P), and x(Q). A minimum spanning tree of their complete graph gives the cut.

Click an edge to follow its lifts

Circles: critical values of x. Diamonds: marked x-coordinates. The dashed ray extends the cut to ∞.

02 Integration paths on C

The coordinate x makes C a two-sheeted cover of ℙ¹. The tree lifts to paths on C, forming the sheet boundaries along which we integrate.

Drag to rotate. Click a colored path to inspect its integrals. This is a projection; crossings need not be intersections on C.

03 The regulator matrix

Stokes’ theorem turns surface integrals on C into path integrals along the lifted boundaries.

The two regulator columns, completed by the period column, give the determinant D.
